# 7. EU / Portugal / Germany mobility synthesis

## Current legal position

At present, the candidate should say:

```text
I am based in Colombia and available for remote contractor/B2B work. I do not currently hold EU work authorization.
```

## Portuguese passport route

The Portuguese passport expected in ~2 years is strategically important, but it is not current authorization.

Once issued, it may unlock:

- right to live and work in Portugal;
- right to work across EU/EEA subject to local registration rules;
- easier EU applications;
- stronger recruiter response for EU roles;
- relocation without employer-sponsored visa in many cases.

Until issued, it should be described carefully:

```text
I expect to obtain Portuguese citizenship/passport in approximately two years, but I am currently Colombia-based and available as a contractor.
```

## Germany route through marriage/residence

Marriage to a German citizen can be a real route if relocation happens legally and the corresponding residence title is granted.

It can potentially unlock:

- residence in Germany;
- work authorization;
- local employment applications;
- access to German/EU employer market;
- more relevance for German language.

It should not be presented as current work authorization.

## Blue Card

The EU Blue Card is an alternative route, but not the primary plan.

It is more relevant if:

- a German/EU employer offers a qualifying salary;
- the degree/qualification is accepted;
- the role fits shortage or skilled-worker requirements;
- relocation is planned.

| Scenario | Statement |
|---|---|
| Now | Colombia-based, available for remote contractor work |
| Applying to US remote | Available as independent contractor from Colombia |

# 15. Interview and negotiation synthesis

## Preparation priorities

Prepare hardest for:

1. explaining TwinSight;
2. Unity optimization;
3. WebGL constraints;
4. CAD-to-realtime pipeline;
5. C# architecture;
6. technical UI;
7. AI-assisted development disclosure;
8. contractor availability;
9. salary expectations.

## Take-home test policy

Accept only if:

- scope is limited;
- time requirement is reasonable;
- no production work is being extracted;
- ownership/IP terms are clear;
- the company is legitimate;
- the role is high enough priority.

Reject or renegotiate if:

- unpaid test exceeds reasonable scope;
- it resembles client work;
- it requires full feature delivery;
- no feedback is promised;
- company avoids contract clarity.

## Negotiation stance

For contractor work:

```text
My expected range depends on scope, duration, time-zone overlap, and whether the role is contractor or employee. For international contractor work, I usually evaluate monthly retainers or hourly rates based on project complexity and expected availability.
```

Do not reveal minimum too early.

## Offer scorecard

Score offers on:

- gross compensation;
- net compensation;
- role fit;
- portfolio leverage;
- contract safety;
- remote stability;
- learning ROI;
- EU leverage;
- payment reliability;
- IP and exclusivity terms.
