# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

International recruiters, hiring managers, technical art leads, Unity/WebGL teams, and 3D pipeline reviewers evaluating Alexander Woodcock Salomon for remote technical 3D work. They scan quickly, then inspect proof when something feels credible.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to communicate a technical-creative profile with conservative claims and strong evidence. TwinSight X500 proves Unity WebGL, C#, CAD-to-realtime optimization, technical UI, interaction, and evaluation thinking. Human Character Pipeline Study proves Blender workflow literacy across sculpting, topology, UVs, materials, grooming, lighting, and rendering. ARA supports tooling and documentation range without becoming the main story.

Success means a recruiter can understand the role fit in under one minute, then inspect deeper proof without feeling oversold or confused.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, and mechanically calm. The site should feel technical and artistic at the same time, with the technical branch clearly leading. It should feel premium and tactile, but never ornamental for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI portfolio patterns: huge modal cards, decorative dots, fake dashboards, repeated uppercase labels, feature-card grids, purple-blue AI glow, over-explained recruiter copy, custom cursor effects, laggy scroll, and interactions that feel like demos instead of useful inspection. Avoid making the profile look like a pure character artist portfolio.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence first: every visual flourish should help a reviewer understand a project faster.
2. Physical-digital tactility: cards and media should feel like objects that can be approached, inspected, and then opened only when needed.
3. Conservative claims: distinguish prototype, study, training context, placeholder, and verified result clearly.
4. Motion with intent: animation should clarify hierarchy, feedback, or inspection flow, never decorate.
5. Living system: content and emphasis should be easy to adapt for different applications.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect WCAG AA contrast, keyboard focus, reduced motion, responsive touch targets, and clear navigation back to the main work list. Avoid scroll or pointer effects that block normal browsing. Every rich interaction needs a straightforward fallback on mobile and reduced-motion settings.
