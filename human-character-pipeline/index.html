Training context

Character pipeline study completed in Blender following CG Cookie HUMAN training material. The final execution, scene setup, grooming/material work and presentation are framed as a portfolio learning piece, not a formal certification or professional character commission.

Pipeline pass

A character study shown as process, not just result.

Each stage answers a production question: form, topology, materials and presentation. The final render matters more when the process is readable.

01

Form before polish

The study starts with sculpting decisions: planes, proportion, facial structure and the kind of observation that keeps later detail grounded.

02

Topology makes it readable

The mesh is treated as evidence. Loops, density and edge flow matter because they reveal how well the asset can survive production use.

03

Materials carry the close-up

Skin, eyes and surface variation are handled as lookdev problems, not only texture slots to fill.

04

Presentation closes the loop

Grooming, lighting and render control make the work legible as a pipeline study rather than a lone final image.
