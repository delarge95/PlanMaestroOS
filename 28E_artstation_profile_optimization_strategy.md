## 6. NoAI Tagging & Visibility Strategy

### How ArtStation’s NoAI tag works

ArtStation’s native **"NoAI"** checkbox adds a metadata signal and policy flag prohibiting collection, aggregation, mining, or scraping of content for testing, inputting, or integrating with AI. Epic Games does not use ArtStation content for generative AI training, and the NoAI tag provides an explicit opt-in protection layer.

### Visibility vs. protection (impact on search ranking)

There is **no documented evidence** that enabling “NoAI” or using “#NoAI” in descriptions reduces your ranking in ArtStation’s internal search, job matching, or recruiter filters. Discoverability still depends on presentation quality, correct tags, and portfolio strength.
