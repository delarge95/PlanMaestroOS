## 2025-03-04 - Dynamic Embed Safe Whitelisting and Restrictive Sandboxing
**Vulnerability:** User-controlled URLs (such as Notion live embeds or video links) could be rendered in dynamic iframes without hostname validation or a restrictive `sandbox` attribute, posing risks of Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), phishing, and open redirection.
**Learning:** In flexible, personalized dashboards that permit user-supplied URLs (e.g., Notion integration workspaces), rendering untrusted URLs directly within iframes exposes users to malicious scripts executing in the context of the client's browser. Furthermore, omitting the `sandbox` attribute allows iframes full capability to navigate parent pages, submit forms, or run unverified scripts.
**Prevention:** Always parse and validate dynamic user-supplied or stored third-party URLs against a strict whitelist of known-safe, HTTPS-only domains using robust URL parsing rather than naive regex matchers. Additionally, employ highly restrictive `sandbox` attributes on all `iframe` elements (e.g., limiting to `allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-presentation allow-forms` depending on requirements) to isolate embedded content.
