This document provides reusable outreach templates for the international job-search system.

It includes English and Spanish versions for:

- recruiter connection messages;
- hiring manager messages;
- technical art lead messages;
- Unity technical director messages;
- XR / simulation / digital twin companies;
- creative technology studios;
- game studio recruiters;
- speculative applications;
- referral requests;
- professor / advisor recommendation requests;
- former freelance client testimonial requests;
- follow-ups;
- salary expectation responses;
- remote-from-Colombia explanations;
- contractor setup explanations;
- future Portuguese / EU mobility explanations;
- contract/payment questions;
- declining unstable offers;
- explaining low formal experience;
- explaining AI-assisted development;
- answering “Why should we hire you?”

The goal is to support consistent, direct, non-desperate, evidence-based communication.

<!-- chunk

section: 1. Messaging principles > Tone
entities:
rules:
-->
Use:

- direct;
- technically specific;
- concise;
- evidence-based;
- no begging;
- no exaggerated enthusiasm;
- no inflated seniority.

Avoid:

- “I am passionate about everything tech”;
- “I would love any opportunity”;
- “I can do anything”;
- long autobiographical explanations;
- overexplaining legal status;
- overexplaining AI usage unless asked.
