# 27_strategy_scenarios_conservative_balanced_aggressive

## Purpose

This file defines three execution scenarios for the international job-search roadmap:

1. **Conservative**

# 3. Conservative strategy

## Definition

The conservative strategy prioritizes stability, lower risk, realistic first employment, and controlled skill growth.

It aims to secure a first relevant role without overexposing the candidate to risky contractor structures, high-pressure international interviews, or overly senior roles.

## Best use case

Use conservative mode if:

- cashflow pressure becomes high;
- portfolio is still incomplete;
- interviews reveal technical gaps;
- confidence in English interviews is low;
- legal/admin contractor setup is not ready;
- there is strong need for stable routine;
- thesis defense or graduation tasks still consume time.

## Primary target roles

| Priority | Role |
|---|---|
| 1 | Unity Developer, junior/early-mid |
| 2 | Interactive 3D Developer |
| 3 | Technical Visualization Developer, junior |
| 4 | Unity WebGL Developer, small team |
| 5 | Python automation role, secondary |
| 6 | Local/LATAM software role, fallback |

## Target markets

| Market | Priority |
|---|---|
| Colombia remote/hybrid | Medium |
| LATAM nearshore | High |
| US small remote companies | Medium |
| Global contractor platforms | Medium |
| EU local roles | Low until EU authorization |
| Germany local roles | Low until legal route active |
| AAA games | Low |
| Large digital twin enterprise | Watchlist |

## Salary target

| Timeframe | Target |
|---|---:|
| First 3 months | USD 1.5k–2.5k/month |
| 3–6 months | USD 2k–3k/month |
| 12 months | USD 3k–4k/month |

## Application volume

| Action | Weekly target |
|---|---:|
| Applications | 10–20 |
| A-priority applications | 3–6 |
| Outreach | 5–10 |
| Recruiter contacts | 5–10 |
| Follow-ups | 5–10 |
| Portfolio blocks | 2–3 |
| Skill blocks | 2–4 |

## Portfolio standard

Minimum required:

- base CV;
- LinkedIn updated;
- TwinSight case study draft;
- GitHub cleaned enough;
- 5–8 screenshots;
- no major public contradictions.

Demo video can be draft-quality.

## Contractor stance

Conservative mode prefers:

- clear written contracts;
- monthly retainer;
- stable payment terms;
- LATAM-friendly clients;
- smaller but safer roles;
- shorter commitments.

Avoid:

- unpaid tests;
- equity-only;
- payment-after-client-approval;
- ambiguous offshore contracts;
- broad IP/exclusivity clauses.

## Advantages

- Lower probability of severe contract/payment problems.
- Easier first win.
- Less interview pressure.
- More room to continue improving portfolio.
- Lower risk of burnout.

## Disadvantages

- Slower compensation growth.
- Higher chance of accepting roles below long-term potential.
- More risk of drifting into generic software/web work.
- Less exposure to high-value US/EU opportunities.

## Conservative red lines

Reject if:

- below USD 1.5k/month unless survival requires it;
- role is unrelated and consumes full-time capacity;
- no written contract;
- no portfolio value;
- broad non-compete;
- payment risk high.

## Conservative success definition

Success after 90 days:

```text
A stable role or active pipeline around USD 1.5k–3k/month that preserves the Unity / real-time 3D / technical visualization direction.
```

# 4. Balanced strategy

## Definition

The balanced strategy combines realistic application volume, strong portfolio positioning, international contractor targeting, and risk control.

It aims to reach the USD 3k/month target within 3–6 months while building toward higher-value roles over 12–24 months.

## Best use case

Use balanced mode if:

- TwinSight case study is publishable;
- LinkedIn/CV/GitHub are coherent;
- English interviews are viable;
- contractor setup can be completed;
- there is no immediate survival-level cashflow pressure;
- the goal is better compensation without reckless risk.

## Portfolio standard

Required:

- polished CV;

- TwinSight case study public or shareable;
- GitHub cleaned;

Balanced mode accepts contractor work if:

- compensation is above minimum;
- payment terms are clear;
- scope is defined;

- gross-to-net estimate works.

## Advantages

- Keeps the profile focused.
- Builds market feedback quickly.

- Preserves 12–24 month growth path.

## Disadvantages

- Requires consistent execution.
- Requires more outreach and rejection tolerance.
- Requires contractor admin readiness.
- Requires stronger interview preparation.

## Balanced red lines

Reject if:

- role is full-stack-only with no 3D/Unity relevance;
- compensation below USD 1.5k/month;
- unpaid production test;
- vague contractor terms;
- company cannot clarify remote/legal structure;
- role consumes full-time capacity while damaging positioning.

## Balanced success definition

Success after 90 days:

```text
A live pipeline of interviews and/or offers in Unity, real-time 3D, technical visualization or WebGL roles, with realistic compensation moving toward USD 3k/month.
```

It assumes more rejection, more competition, and higher contract risk.

## Best use case

Use aggressive mode if:

- TwinSight case study is strong;
- demo video is polished;
- GitHub and LinkedIn are clean;
- interview answers are rehearsed;
- contractor/tax setup is ready;
- cashflow pressure is manageable;
- the candidate can tolerate rejection and uncertainty;
- target is USD 4k–6k+/month as soon as possible.

# 7. Scenario triggers

## Switch to conservative if

- portfolio is not ready after 30 days;
- cashflow pressure increases;
- interview rejection shows strong technical gaps;
- contractor setup is not legally/admin ready;
- thesis defense demands attention;
- mental load becomes unsustainable;
- application volume collapses.

## Stay balanced if

- applications are being sent consistently;
- some recruiter responses appear;
- portfolio is improving weekly;
- technical gaps are manageable;
- salary conversations are near target;
- rejection is normal but not total silence.

## Switch to aggressive if

- TwinSight gets strong feedback;
- portfolio/demo is polished;
- recruiter response rate is above 6–10%;
- interviews are happening;
- contractor setup is clear;
- USD 3k+ conversations are plausible;
- top A-priority companies show fit.

# 8. Hybrid recommended strategy

## Best practical model

The optimal path is not purely conservative, balanced or aggressive.

Recommended hybrid:

```text
Balanced strategy as default.
Aggressive targeting for A-priority companies.
Conservative contract-risk controls.
```

## What this means

Use **balanced volume**:

```text
15–30 applications/week
10–25 outreach messages/week
```

Use **aggressive targeting** for:

- technical visualization companies;
- Unity WebGL roles;
- digital twin visualization;
- simulation interfaces;
- product configurators;
- XR-adjacent Unity roles;
- global remote studios.

Use **conservative rules** for:

- contract review;
- payment terms;
- legal status;
- IP ownership;
- unpaid tests;
- relocation;
- contractor setup.

## Hybrid operating statement

```text
Apply like an ambitious international contractor.
Negotiate like a risk-aware professional.
Position like a focused Unity technical visualization specialist.
```

# 11. Scenario-specific salary stance

## Conservative salary stance

Use when:

- role is first relevant job;
- company is LATAM/local;
- project fit is strong but budget moderate.

Language:

```text
For remote contractor work, I’m open to discussing compensation based on scope, expected availability and contract structure. My priority is a role with strong fit in Unity, real-time 3D or technical visualization.
```

Do not anchor too high if the role is clearly lower-budget.

## Balanced salary stance

Use by default.

Language:

```text
For remote contractor work, depending on scope and expected availability, I’m generally targeting the USD 2k–4.5k/month range or an equivalent hourly rate.
```

Adjust per role.

## Aggressive salary stance

Use only for strong-fit US/EU/global roles.

Language:

```text
For a full-time remote contractor arrangement involving Unity, WebGL, technical visualization and real-time 3D systems, I would generally evaluate compensation in the USD 4k–6k/month range depending on scope, ownership and contract structure.
```

Do not use aggressive anchors for low-budget LATAM roles.
